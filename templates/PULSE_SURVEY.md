# Anonymous Community Pulse Survey

**Status:** Suggested template. Collect without names; avoid collecting identifiable metadata if anonymity is promised.

Rate each statement from **1 (strongly disagree)** to **5 (strongly agree)**, or skip it.

1. I feel comfortable disagreeing with influential members. [1–5 / skip]
2. I can share something personal without feeling judged. [1–5 / skip]
3. I can decline an activity without harming friendships. [1–5 / skip]
4. Group decisions feel fair and transparent. [1–5 / skip]
5. Being part of this group adds something meaningful to my life. [1–5 / skip]

## Optional free-text prompts
- What should we keep doing?
- What should we change?
- Is there anything making it harder to speak freely?

## Handling results
Share aggregated themes only. In a very small group, even anonymous free text may identify someone; do not publish raw responses without consent. Never use scores to rank members.
