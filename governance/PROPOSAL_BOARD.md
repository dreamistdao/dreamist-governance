# Proposal Board Guide

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Suggested columns
`Ideas → Discussion → Trial → Adopted` with `Deferred / Rejected` for proposals that do not proceed.

## Minimum proposal fields
- ID and title
- Proposer
- Problem or opportunity
- Proposed change
- People affected
- Effort, cost, and risks
- Proposed trial period
- Volunteer coordinator
- Decision and unresolved objections
- Review date

## Board rules
Anyone can submit. The coordinator organizes; they do not gatekeep ideas. Members may comment asynchronously. Do not include private personal stories. Record why a proposal moved stages and when a trial expires.
