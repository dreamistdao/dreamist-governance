# Dreamist DAO

**A brotherhood built on trust, openness, personal growth, and shared responsibility.**

> Status: Draft for member review. No rule is binding until members agree to it.

## Why we exist
We want a small group of friends where everyone can speak honestly, disagree safely, learn from one another, and build lasting relationships without judgment or pressure. Our goal is not to create another hierarchy or formal business club.

## Our operating principles
- Equal voice does not require equal speaking time; everyone must have a genuine opportunity to contribute.
- Silence is **not** consent.
- Sharing is optional; passing is respected.
- Confidential personal experiences stay out of public notes.
- Facilitation rotates; influence does not grant special authority.
- Keep processes light, test them, and revise them together.

## What DAO means here
We use DAO as shorthand for a **community governed collectively**. We do not currently require blockchain, tokens, treasury, or on-chain voting. If we later introduce funds, legal entities, or on-chain tools, those need separate explicit approval and appropriate legal review.
