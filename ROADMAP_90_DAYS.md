# First 90 Days

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Month 1 — Trust first
- Hold an orientation and jointly revise the charter.
- Agree on confidentiality, right to pass, and respectful disagreement.
- Try the first monthly meeting.
- Gather private feedback on whether everyone feels heard.

## Month 2 — Lightweight governance
- Rotate the facilitator.
- Open a shared proposal board.
- Trial consent-based decisions for reversible changes.
- Organize one optional bonding activity.

## Month 3 — Review and adapt
- Continue monthly meetings and rotate responsibilities.
- Collect an anonymous pulse survey.
- Review unresolved tensions and meeting burden.
- Revise or discontinue processes that do not help.
- Formally decide which policies, if any, to adopt.

## Success criteria
Members can disagree without fear, feel free to pass, understand decisions, and genuinely enjoy the group. Do not judge success by attendance alone.

## Day-90 retrospective prompts
What strengthened trust? When did we feel pressured? Which process was useful? What should we stop, start, or keep? What rules do we actually want to adopt?
