Intent

A monthly rhythm for useful friendship.

This is a place to be honest about what is working, difficult, or still unclear—and to contribute what you know to friends who may need it. It is not a presentation contest or a corporate meeting.

Participation is flexible. You may attend, share, listen, help, or pass. All are valid ways to participate.

Suggested agenda · 90 minutes

A clear shape, with room to adapt.

Adjust sharing and Deep Dive time for the number of people present. Protect social time before or after the agenda when possible.

Common sharing flow

A shared flow. Your own presentation style.

The flow is shared; the presentation style is not. Move through these prompts in your own way.

01

Current focus

What are you working on or thinking about in business, work, family, health, learning, habits, or personal growth?

02

Wins & lessons

What went well? What failed but taught you something?

03

Challenges

What is taking too much time, energy, money, or attention?

04

Personal growth

What habit, routine, idea, or change are you trying?

05

Ask

Would feedback, experience, an introduction, a resource, or simply listening help? You may say, “I’m not looking for advice; I just want to share.”

06

Offer

What knowledge, resources, connections, or experience can you offer the group?

Choose your format

Slides are a tool—not a requirement.

Use slides, notes, a whiteboard, a demo, photos, or simply talk.

Optional slide structure

  1. What I’m focused on
  2. Wins and lessons
  3. Deep dive or current challenge
  4. Personal life and growth
  5. What I need and what I can offer
  6. Ideas for the community (optional)

Aim for about 10 minutes per person. If the group is large, rotate longer sharing slots between meetings.

Deep Dive

Slow down for the topic that needs more room.

Use this when a business or personal subject deserves more than a quick update.

Do not jump straight to solutions.

Make room for the presenter’s context and for the group’s lived experience. The presenter remains the owner of their next step.

01Explain the situation and why it matters.
02Ask clarifying questions.
03Listen and understand before suggesting solutions.
04Share relevant personal experiences—not prescriptions.
05Reflect on what was useful and choose the next step.

Guiding sequence: listen → clarify → understand → share relevant experience → let the person decide.

Meeting guidelines

Trust is the infrastructure.

These practices protect honest conversation, voluntary participation, and the friendship underneath the work.

01

Sharing and sensitive disclosure are voluntary; listening and helping are valid participation.

02

Share what is real now. Wins and failures are both useful.

03

Ask clearly and say what kind of help you want.

04

Give as well as receive, when you genuinely can.

05

Prefer “Here is what happened when I faced something similar” over “You should do this.”

06

Ask before giving advice. Challenge ideas, not people, and do not interrupt.

07

What is shared in the circle stays in the circle. No recording or sharing screenshots, notes, messages, personal stories, internal company information, or financial details without informed permission.

08

Take away your own learning, but ask permission before repeating another member’s story.

09

Do not compare status, income, company size, or connections, and do not use the meeting for unsolicited selling.

10

Keep governance separate from confidential personal sharing.

11

Periodically use Keep / Change / Try to improve the format together.

After the meeting

Capture the useful, protect the personal.

Publish only approved action items, governance outcomes, and non-identifying process feedback. Do not summarize private stories. Confirm the date and rotating host or facilitator for the next monthly meeting.
