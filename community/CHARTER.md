# Community Charter

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Mission
Build a small, trusted brotherhood of friends who help one another grow through honest conversation, mutual respect, shared experiences, and practical support.

## Vision
A place where members can say what they truly think—even when they disagree with the most influential person in the room—without fear of humiliation, retaliation, or exclusion.

## Values
1. **Trust:** We handle one another's stories with care.
2. **Equal dignity:** No member's status, wealth, experience, or personality makes their voice more valuable.
3. **Nonjudgment:** We seek understanding rather than instant evaluation.
4. **Growth:** We share useful experiences, lessons, and perspectives.
5. **Freedom:** Attendance, personal sharing, and help are voluntary unless a specific commitment was freely accepted.
6. **Accountability:** We follow through on commitments and acknowledge mistakes.
7. **Friendship first:** Process exists to strengthen relationships, not dominate them.

## What we are not
- Not a competition for achievement, money, or influence.
- Not a therapy service or substitute for professional help.
- Not an investment club, company, or token-governed organization by default.
- Not a place where disagreement is treated as disloyalty.

## Success looks like
Members feel heard, can respectfully disagree, trust that private stories stay private, enjoy spending time together, and leave interactions with new understanding or encouragement.

## Review
Discuss this charter together and revisit after 90 days, then at least every six months.
