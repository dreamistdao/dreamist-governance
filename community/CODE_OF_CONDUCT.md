# Brotherhood Agreement & Code of Conduct

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Proposed commitments
1. Listen to understand; do not interrupt or dismiss.
2. Critique ideas and actions, not a person's worth.
3. Ask before giving advice: “Would you like ideas, or would you prefer us to listen?”
4. Never pressure someone to disclose private information.
5. Members may pass, decline activities, or step away without needing to justify it.
6. Disagreement is welcome. Ridicule, intimidation, bullying, discrimination, and retaliation are not.
7. Protect confidentiality according to our separate trust policy.
8. Give everyone a chance to contribute, especially quieter members, without forcing them to speak.
9. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest in group decisions.
10. Keep commitments realistic and communicate when plans change.

## If a boundary is crossed
- The facilitator may pause the conversation and restate the agreed rule.
- Anyone may request a break or a private discussion with the trust steward or another mutually acceptable member.
- Seek clarification and repair first when safe and appropriate.
- For repeated or serious violations, members should follow an agreed fair review process, including an opportunity to respond and appropriate safeguards for those affected.
- Do not promise absolute confidentiality where immediate safety or legal obligations require action.

## Open questions before adoption
- How should repeated violations be reviewed?
- What decision threshold should apply to membership suspension or removal?
- Who facilitates a concern involving the current facilitator or trust steward?

**This document is a proposal, not an adopted disciplinary policy.**
