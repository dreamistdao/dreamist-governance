# Trust, Privacy & Confidentiality

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## The default rule
Personal stories shared in the group stay in the group. Do not forward screenshots, quote members outside the group, or identify who shared a story without explicit permission.

## Distinguish two information types
**Private sharing:** personal, family, emotional, career, or financial experiences. Do not record these in shared meeting notes.

**Governance information:** proposals, decisions, assigned voluntary actions, and review dates. These may be documented for members.

## Respectful sharing
- Ask whether someone wants listening, questions, or advice.
- Speak from personal experience rather than diagnosing or judging others.
- Do not repeat a private story to an absent member unless the speaker agrees.
- Avoid recording sessions; any exception requires informed consent from everyone involved.
- Do not upload private stories into AI tools or third-party services without informed permission.

## Limits
Confidentiality is an important group norm, but not an absolute guarantee. Members should agree in advance how to respond to credible immediate safety risks, abuse, or applicable legal obligations, prioritizing appropriate help and minimum necessary disclosure.

## Repair
If private information is shared improperly, notify the affected member promptly, stop further distribution where possible, and agree on appropriate remediation.
