# New Member Orientation

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## 30-minute orientation
1. **5 min — Why Dreamist DAO exists:** friends, trust, openness, growth.
2. **5 min — What it is not:** no status contest, compulsory sharing, or permanent leader.
3. **8 min — Community agreement:** listening, confidentiality, disagreement, boundaries.
4. **5 min — How it works:** monthly meetings, the sharing flow, rotating roles, and proposals.
5. **5 min — Practice:** each person can share a small preference or disagreement; passing is welcome.
6. **2 min — Questions and next steps.**

## Suggested opening script
“Dreamist DAO is a group of friends learning to grow together. Nobody needs to impress anyone here. You can disagree with me or anyone else, and you can choose not to share. Our goal is to build enough trust that honesty feels safe.”

## New member checklist
- [ ] Read the charter and community agreement.
- [ ] Discuss confidentiality and its limits.
- [ ] Understand optional participation and the right to pass.
- [ ] Know where proposals and meeting schedules live.
- [ ] Know how to raise a concern privately.
- [ ] Have an opportunity to suggest changes before accepting group rules.

## Admission
The group should explicitly agree on invitation and membership processes before adopting any membership restrictions. No single person should unilaterally control membership without a rule the group has approved.
