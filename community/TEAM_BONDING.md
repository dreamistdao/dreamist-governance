# Team Bonding & Friendship

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Purpose
Friendship should exist outside meetings and proposals. Shared enjoyment, informal conversation, and mutual care build trust without turning every interaction into a productivity exercise.

## Suggested rhythm
- **Biweekly:** optional catchup and learning exchange.
- **Monthly:** optional casual activity (meal, board games, walk, sports, creative activity).
- **Quarterly:** group reflection or longer get-together if members want it.

## Principles
- Rotate hosts; allow people to suggest activities.
- Agree on costs before booking; offer low-cost options.
- Respect different schedules, abilities, and comfort levels.
- No attendance penalties, guilt, or forced disclosure.
- Do not turn bonding into hidden performance assessment.

## Simple activity ideas
Shared meal; show-and-tell of personal projects; collaborative cooking; a game night; a short learning workshop; a member-hosted hobby introduction.

## Check success
Ask whether members enjoyed the time and felt included, not whether attendance was perfect.
