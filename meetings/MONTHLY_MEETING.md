# Monthly Meeting Playbook

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Intent
A monthly, relaxed gathering where friends can share what is happening in business, work, and life; learn from one another; ask for help; and offer support. It should be structured enough to be useful and informal enough to remain a friendship. Attendance, presenting, and personal sharing are optional.

## Suggested 90-minute agenda
| Time | Activity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 min | Opening and check-in | What changed this month? Each member may share or pass |
| 10–50 min | Member sharing | Use the common sharing flow below; about 10 minutes per volunteer |
| 50–75 min | Deep Dive | Explore one useful business or personal topic |
| 75–85 min | Group matters | Approved proposals or improvements to the meeting format only |
| 85–90 min | Closing | Asks, offers, appreciation, optional next steps, and next host |

Adjust the sharing and Deep Dive time to fit the number of people present. Protect social time before or after the agenda when possible.

## Common sharing flow
The flow is shared; the presentation style is not.

1. **Current focus:** What are you working on or thinking about in business, work, family, health, learning, habits, or personal growth?
2. **Wins and lessons:** What went well? What failed but taught you something?
3. **Challenges or roadblocks:** What is taking too much time, energy, money, or attention?
4. **Personal growth:** What habit, routine, idea, or change are you trying?
5. **Ask:** Would feedback, experience, an introduction, a resource, or simply listening help? You may say, “I’m not looking for advice; I just want to share.”
6. **Offer:** What knowledge, resources, connections, or experience can you offer the group?

Members may use slides, notes, a whiteboard, a demo, photos, or simply talk. A useful optional slide structure is:

1. What I’m focused on
2. Wins and lessons
3. Deep dive or current challenge
4. Personal life and growth
5. What I need and what I can offer
6. Ideas for the community (optional)

Aim for about 10 minutes per person. Slides are a tool, not a requirement. If the group is large, rotate longer sharing slots between meetings.

## Deep Dive
When a topic needs more than a quick update:

1. The member explains the situation and why it matters.
2. The group asks clarifying questions.
3. Everyone listens and understands before suggesting solutions.
4. Members share relevant personal experiences rather than prescriptions.
5. The presenter reflects on what was useful and chooses their own next step.

The guiding sequence is: **listen → clarify → understand → share relevant experience → let the person decide.**

## Meeting guidelines
- Sharing and sensitive disclosure are voluntary; listening and helping are valid participation.
- Share what is real now. Wins and failures are both useful.
- Ask clearly and say what kind of help you want.
- Give as well as receive, when you genuinely can.
- Prefer “Here is what happened when I faced something similar” over “You should do this.”
- Ask before giving advice. Challenge ideas, not people, and do not interrupt.
- What is shared in the circle stays in the circle. No recording or sharing screenshots, notes, messages, personal stories, internal company information, or financial details without informed permission.
- Take away your own learning, but ask permission before repeating another member’s story.
- Do not compare status, income, company size, or connections, and do not use the meeting for unsolicited selling.
- Keep governance separate from confidential personal sharing.
- Periodically use **Keep / Change / Try** to improve the format together.

## After the meeting
Publish only approved action items, governance outcomes, and non-identifying process feedback. Do not summarize private stories. Confirm the date and rotating host or facilitator for the next monthly meeting.
