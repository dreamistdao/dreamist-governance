# Biweekly Catchup Playbook

**Status:** Discussion Draft v0.1 · 20 October 2026

## Intent
A recurring, relaxed gathering for personal growth, mutual understanding, and lightweight group coordination. Attendance and personal sharing are optional.

## Suggested 90-minute agenda
| Time | Activity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 min | Check-in | Each member may share or pass |
| 10–40 min | Personal sharing | Wins, struggles, lessons; ask before advice |
| 40–60 min | Learning exchange | Topic, resource, or experience from a volunteer |
| 60–80 min | Proposal discussion | Separate from confidential personal sharing |
| 80–90 min | Closing | Appreciation, optional next steps, next host |

## Sharing prompts
- What stage am I at in life or work?
- What went well recently?
- What challenge am I navigating?
- What useful habit or lesson have I learned?
- Is there any support I would like to request?

Members may use four slides, simple notes, or speak informally. Ten minutes per person is a guideline, not a requirement. If the group is larger, rotate longer sharing slots.

## Ground rules
No forced sharing; no recording by default; no unsolicited problem-solving; no interruptions; protect private details; discuss governance separately.

## After the meeting
Publish only approved action items and governance outcomes. Do not summarize private stories.
